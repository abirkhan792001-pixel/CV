## What this version does differently

You asked for something simple and straightforward that leads with what you
built and what the result was. So every one of the first three paragraphs now
opens on a thing you made and closes on a number, and the reflective material
that used to sit between them is gone.

**The order is deliberate.** SQRlane goes first because the posting asks for
expert knowledge of AI frameworks and for consulting clients on AI adoption, and
your auditability argument is the single strongest sentence you have against that
requirement — it is a point most applicants cannot make at all. SCAILE follows
because it is the cleanest built-this / got-that pair on your record. A&M is
third because it is the consulting credential, and it is also where PowerPoint
and Excel get covered without reading as a checklist.

**Length: 491 words**, salutation to sign-off, which is one page once the
address block and signature are in. The previous draft was 755 on the same
measure, or about two pages. A German Anschreiben is conventionally one page and
recruiters do notice.

## What I took out, and why

- **Biome, entirely.** The letter was carrying four employers and could not
  afford a fourth. Biome is also the weakest entry against this posting, and the
  README's own warning applies to it — see the employment-basis note below.
- **"One of 400 selected."** Without a denominator this reads as a large
  programme rather than a selective one, which is the opposite of what you
  wanted it to do. It now says "in partnership with the European Commission",
  which is exactly what your CV says. If you know the number it was selected
  from, tell me and I will put the stronger version back.
- **The gap-year sentence.** The old draft had a placeholder for 08.2024 to
  07.2025. I left it out rather than leave a hole in the letter. A one-page
  cover letter is not where a gap gets explained, and raising it unprompted
  spends words drawing attention to it. The README already flags that German
  recruiters read timelines closely and will ask, so have the answer ready for
  the form and the interview. **If you want a line in the letter anyway, tell me
  what you were doing and I will add one sentence** — it slots in at the start of
  the final paragraph.
- **"A preference for the hardest problem in the room."** It asserted something
  the three paragraphs above it already demonstrate. Claims like that cost
  credibility when the evidence is sitting right there.

## Two things to check before you send

1. **The employment-basis line.** The posting asks for a minimum of one year in
   operational business or management consulting **as a full-time employee**. The
   letter says "my consulting work was at Alvarez & Marsal" and states the
   workstream and the dates are on the CV. It does not claim full-time
   employment, and it must not — the README is right that a stated claim which
   fails a reference check is a different order of problem from a title that
   omits a qualifier. Keep that wording as it is.
2. **VivaTech, Siemens and Audi.** That sentence is from your own material but it
   is not on the CV, so it is the one line in the letter a reader holding both
   documents will see for the first time. That is fine — cover letters add detail
   — but be ready to talk about it.

## Still open

The **$10M saving** and **shaped the fund's investment philosophy** claims stay
out. Your CV says a $9.8M plan and a pre-existing fund thesis, and the letter now
agrees with it, which is what you want when a reader has both in front of them.
