## 1. Fix "one of 400" before anything else

As written this works against you. Without a denominator, *one of 400* reads as a
large programme, not a selective one - the reader's instinct is "so 400 people
got this." You wanted it to signal difficulty and it currently signals the
opposite. Three ways out:

- **Give the denominator:** *one of 400 selected from [X] applications*. Best
  option if you know the number.
- **Give the scope:** *one of only 400 projects the Commission partners across
  Europe each year*. "Only" plus "across Europe" makes 400 read as small. Use
  this only if that scope is accurate.
- **Drop the number:** *My thesis, written in partnership with the European
  Commission, looks at...* Loses the selectivity claim but loses nothing else,
  and never backfires.

I left your 400 in the text because it is your call, but I would not send it in
its current form.
